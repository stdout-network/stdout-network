# Treasury

Monthly summaries go here as `YYYY-MM.md`, in aggregate only:

- Revenue from advertisers
- Developer share paid out (total, and how much was redirected to open source projects and charities)
- Ads-for-good impressions served
- Network costs
- Profit, and how it was split: fighting fund (10%), beer pot, marketing pot
- Fighting fund: grants made this quarter (count and total) and the balance

Never publish individual earnings, and never anything about who asked the fighting fund for help.

Fighting fund requests go privately to the fund panel's address, never through issues, PRs or public channels.
