# RFCs

An RFC is how a cross-cutting idea becomes a decision: a new product, a protocol or privacy change, a policy, a new commune.

1. Copy `0000-template.md` to `NNNN-short-title.md` (next free number) and open a PR.
2. Tag the communes it affects. Their co-chairs facilitate.
3. Discussion runs at least 7 days, on the PR.
4. Consensus is the goal. A blocking objection must name the principle or harm it protects and what would resolve it.
5. If still blocked after 14 days, the Confederal Council votes. Social Contract, splits, licence and legal-home changes go to the Assembly instead.
6. On a decision, merge the RFC with its status updated and add a record to `decisions/`.
