# Members

Adding your line here is how you sign the [Charter](CHARTER.md). Open a PR, and the member who brought you in (or a co-chair of the commune that welcomed you, once the door opens) approves it. Founding members just add themselves.

Declaring your employer is required; it is how we apply the one-third rule on the Council (Social Contract, section 6). Pick a commune whenever you're ready.
