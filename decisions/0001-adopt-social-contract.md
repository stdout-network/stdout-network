# Decision 0001: Adopt the Social Contract

- **Date:** (Founding Assembly)
- **Decided by:** Assembly
- **Method:** vote (for / against / abstain)
- **Links:** FOUNDING_ASSEMBLY.md, ballot 1

## Decision

The founding members adopt SOCIAL_CONTRACT.md as amended at the Founding Assembly, and the outcomes of ballots 2 to 10.

## Why

To give the project a structure where power starts in small communes, is shared by co-chairs, and cannot be captured by any one person or employer.

## Consequences

- Communes listed in COMMUNES.md are constituted and their co-chairs seated.
- The Confederal Council holds its first meeting within 14 days.
- Ballot results for the balance rule, licence, splits, ads for good, legal home, name, seats and the fighting fund are recorded in decisions 0002 to 0010.
